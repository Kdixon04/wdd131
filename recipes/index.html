Apple Crisp

⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ☆

4 out of 5 stars

This classic apple crisp is warm, comforting, and easy to make. Tender apples bake beneath a buttery oat topping for a delicious dessert everyone will enjoy.

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 45 minutes

Serves: 6 people

Ingredients

  • 6 medium apples, peeled and sliced
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
